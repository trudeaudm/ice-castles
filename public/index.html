The Story of Castle Quest

Castle Quest is a winter adventure: discover how five elements—Water, Earth, Fire, Air, and Spirit—help bring an Ice Castle to life.

Five Guardians guide the quest. Each watches over an element and offers a challenge to awaken it.

  • Cascade notices water’s smallest wonders
  • Granite remembers the ground beneath the ice
  • Ember finds balance in cold and light
  • Summit follows the wind
  • Aurora gathers the memories people carry away.

Guests awaken the five elements in any order with help from their Guardians, then visit the Winter Keeper Throne to confirm the quest is complete.
